# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the AsciiWall community library project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, creative feedback, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical and curation feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community, artwork provenance integrity, and user safety

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical address, email address, or private model/subject permission proof, without explicit permission
- Submitting undisclosed security exploits, malicious media payloads, or unconsented third-party personal media in public threads
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

During the current bootstrap phase, repository maintainer `@NeerajCodz` (`https://github.com/NeerajCodz`) is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards of acceptable behavior and taking appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, recipes, documentation, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all AsciiWall Library community spaces — including the `Astraive/asciiwall-library` repository, issues, pull requests, review threads, and related project interactions — and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainer (`@NeerajCodz`) before posting anything publicly:

- **Private maintainer contact:** email Neeraj Sathish Kumar (`@NeerajCodz`) at `neerajcodz@gmail.com`
- **Undisclosed security vulnerabilities:** do **not** use general conduct channels; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report via [GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting](https://github.com/Astraive/asciiwall-library/security/advisories/new)
- **Rights and attribution disputes:** follow the takedown process in [docs/licensing-and-review.md](docs/licensing-and-review.md#rights-disputes-and-takedown-process) without posting personal permission evidence publicly

Never post private harassment evidence, personal identifiers, or unredacted host paths in a public issue or pull request. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, respecting the privacy and security of the reporter.
